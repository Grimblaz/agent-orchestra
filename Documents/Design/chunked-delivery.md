# Chunked delivery: design to the seams, plan to the contract

Ratified 2026-07-26 (issue #920, process outcome of the #848 tree review). The operative summary lives in [CLAUDE.md § Chunked delivery](../../CLAUDE.md); this document carries the full doctrine and rationale.

> **Unfamiliar with a term?** `Bound 1 / Bound 2`, `A1`–`A5`, and `DA{N}` are all defined in the [plain-language vocabulary](../../HOW-IT-WORKS.md#vocab).

## Why

Shift-left works on **specification defects** — requirements, contracts, interfaces — where analysis is cheaper than rework. It does not extend to **operational unknowns**: how software actually behaves once it runs. Those are discovered cheapest by running real code, not by deeper analysis. Chunked delivery takes both benefits — waterfall's coherent upstream decisions made once, iterative development's ground truth between decisions — by bounding **where detail is allowed to live** at two distinct levels. Both bounds are load-bearing; enforcing only one recreates the waterfall failure mode one level down.

The evidence behind this: in the #848 tree, churn concentrated where sub-issues of an already-designed parent re-entered the full upstream pipeline (#901 reached NO PLAN after multiple full adversarial panels before its later approved redesign), and where analysis was applied to failure modes of software with zero live runs (#910/#912). The cheapest catches in the tree were live probes (#871, #898).

## The two bounds

**Bound 1 — the parent design stops at the seams.** A parent issue carries the experience framing and technical design once, to a bounded depth: the design decides the **boundaries between implementation chunks** — interfaces, data shapes, spanning invariants, and the chunk sequence — and deliberately does **not** design any chunk's internals. If the design is specifying mechanism inside a chunk, it has gone too deep.

**Bound 2 — the chunk plan is a contract, not a recipe.** Each chunk's **brief** — the declared `plan-variant: brief` plan shape whose six-section contract lives in [`skills/plan-authoring/SKILL.md`](../../skills/plan-authoring/SKILL.md) § Brief plan variant — hands the executor the problem and the evidence for it, a provenance-marked epistemic map, behaviour-pinned acceptance criteria each carrying its own proof standard, the falsifiers the stress-test found, the context the criteria do not say, and the evidence obligations — and stops there. Unknowns *inside* the chunk (mechanism choices, internal structure, how to make the targets pass) belong to the executor's run; the planner must **not** pre-solve them. Plan-phase discovery is read-only grounding sufficient to write checkable targets and honest halt conditions — the moment planning turns into designing the implementation, it has crossed the boundary. The one exception: an unknown that could void a target, invariant, or the chunk boundary itself is not an in-box unknown — surface it as a design gap rather than resolving it unilaterally.

## Operating rules

- **Chunks are plan-only sub-issues.** A chunk sub-issue of a designed parent goes straight to the planner in brief mode — no worth-it check, no experience phase, no design phase, and no standards-check re-litigation of the parent's decisions; the chunk inherits them. One chunk = one sub-issue = one brief = one run = one pull request (PR). Do not split chunks at the PR level under a single issue: the harness's plan marker, run-state, and halt plumbing are all issue-scoped.
- **Inheritance is loaded, not assumed.** The chunk sub-issue body must link its parent, and the planner's first act in chunk mode is reading the parent's design — seams, spanning invariants, and any recorded amendments — before authoring the contract; the chunk's brief cites the parent decisions it implements. Without this, `upstream-onboarding` would synthesize context from the child's own body (which carries no design markers) and the plan could silently violate parent seams.
- **Design gaps route up, not sideways.** If a chunk cannot be planned without a new design decision, that is a design gap on the **parent** — recorded there as a single design amendment — not a design phase on the child. This upward channel is the iterative discovery mechanism: running code corrects the design without re-running the design.
- **Walking skeleton first.** The first chunk is the thinnest end-to-end path through all the seams, so boundary errors surface at chunk-1 prices instead of chunk-5 prices.
- **Every chunk lands in an acceptable resting state.** Each chunk leaves the tree **lawful** (the guidance permits what it says it permits), **honest** (no surface describes absent machinery as live), and **operable** (no surface a reader or agent follows dead-ends — a truthful "pending" with nowhere to go fails this limb) if every remaining chunk is deferred indefinitely. Two chunks that cannot satisfy this separately are one chunk. (Ratified as doctrine by #957, decision `d-resting-state-rule`, 2026-08-02, citing #920/#936; #957 holds the amendment authority for this rule. It governs both entrances — a chunk sequence under a designed parent, and the novel arm of the open-for-work flow, whose chunk output [open-for-work.md](open-for-work.md) binds to it.)
- **A brief takes design review's charter.** The full five-pass plan panel does not *relax* for a brief — it stops applying, because the brief is a design-shaped artifact rather than a step-bearing one. What applies instead is the **brief conformance check** over five properties — four near-mechanical text ones (artifact pinning, provenance marking, proof standards present, suite floor checked) plus one **vacuity reading** of the criteria taken together (no reading under which every criterion passes and no work happens, #957 D2) — and the **routing-call review target**, addressed on *every* brief review, with the arm chosen by the brief's **authority source** and never by whether a verdict happens to be present: a chunk of a designed parent (source (a)) carries no routing verdict of its own and is recorded not-applicable *with that basis stated*, never skipped; a standalone brief (source (b)) must carry a **routine** verdict consistent with its own epistemic map, and the remaining states map to closed outcomes — routine but map-inconsistent is a **finding**; **novel** and **absent** are each a **review failure**, since neither authorizes a brief at all — and none of the three is ever a not-applicable — plus the prosecution-only `design-challenge` shape — three lenses, no defense, no judge, whose convergence cold read carries a required vacuity question on a brief target. The shell performing that cold read is instructed to answer it, and the answer is persisted in **both polarities** into the review's `**Plan Stress-Test**` summary, so a record silent about vacuity reads as nonconforming rather than clean (`skills/plan-authoring/SKILL.md` § Brief plan variant, dispatched from `commands/plan.md` and that skill's stress-test step). Code review's `standard` adapter is untouched by this: the change is adapter *selection* at the dispatch points, never a pass-count edit to a shared adapter. Any *further* movement in review depth — for a brief or for any other plan shape — is still earned by phase-containment-ledger evidence, never by a cost argument (consistent with CLAUDE.md § Quality-first, shift-left); annotate findings in the ledger with whether each was catchable only at plan time. **The caveat that used to sit here is resolved** (#951, chunk #956; DA6 amended to closed on #936). It read: the ledger's plan-surface writer is judge-gated, this charter removes the judge stage for a brief, so a brief renders as an unverified gap rather than as evidence — the instrument this paragraph names was not collecting for the lane this paragraph relaxes. A brief's review now emits lawfully as `caught_stage: brief-review`, authorized by its own judge-free `brief_dispositions:` head carrying a required convergence-filter assertion (`skills/plan-authoring/SKILL.md` § Brief-review emission), and the escape-rate rollup reports the plan arm **partitioned by adjudication standard** so a judge-adjudicated finding and a prosecution-panel-plus-filter finding never pool into one number. **The depth still holds, and for a sharper reason than before**: the instrument is now collecting for this lane, but collecting is not the same as having collected. Relaxation is earned by what the partitioned brief-review arm eventually shows, and that arm starts empty — a sub-arm below the sufficiency floor renders WITHHELD, never a permissive zero.

These operating rules govern the chunk entrance — a brief's authority source (a). A brief has a second lawful authority source outside chunked delivery: a **standalone issue carrying an affirmed open-for-work framing record** (#957 D4), whose doctrine lives in [open-for-work.md](open-for-work.md). The rules above are unchanged by it, and those of them that speak about the brief itself — including the review-charter rule — apply to a brief under either source; the amendments below likewise bind a brief under either source.

## How a chunk plan specifies — amendments A1–A5

Ratified 2026-07-28 (issue #936, from the #932 paired-trial record). Bound 2 says a chunk plan states the contract and stops. These five rules say **at what knowledge level** it is allowed to state it, and **what it must say about proof**. They bind **every brief, whichever authority source it carries** — a chunk sub-issue of a designed parent, or a standalone issue with an affirmed open-for-work framing record (#957 D4).

> **Scope restatement, recorded deliberately (#957 D4; owner disposition 2026-08-02).** The previous wording here bound "every chunk plan regardless of which artifact carries it." The restatement above widens the scope on the authority axis — a standalone brief is now bound — and **narrows it on the artifact axis**: a chunk plan carried by a non-brief artifact (the frame-spine shape — *available but no longer mandated* for migration-type work, per the owner decision of 2026-08-02 recorded in `skills/plan-authoring/SKILL.md` § Brief plan variant; a migration brief carries the exhaustive scan as an acceptance criterion instead) is no longer bound by these amendments on this text. The narrowing is intended, not an oversight: the frame-spine shape is a docketed retirement candidate under #953 and is not to be designed around. If that retirement is ever reverted — it is evidence-gated and explicitly revocable — this scope is the thing to revisit. The section keeps its historical heading ("How a chunk plan specifies") because two cross-references cite it as an anchor; the binding scope is stated by the sentences here, not by the heading.

The trial exposed a third category of unknown that the two bounds do not name. Bound 2's dichotomy is *specification* (planner-owned) versus *in-box mechanism* (executor-owned). **Discoverable external facts** are neither: specification-shaped, but not authorable — only readable from the world. How a vendor tool names its directories is the worked example. A plan that guesses one and folds the guess into a target grades the run against a false premise, and the run has no way to notice.

**A1 — Discovery-neutral targets.**
*Forbidden:* encoding a hypothesis about a discoverable external fact into a target — most often as a comparison tolerance chosen to fit what a handful of samples suggested.
*Required instead:* when done-ness depends on such a fact, the target states two things — the **evidence standard** for establishing it (an authoritative source; a sample inference is not sufficient) and the **behavior once established**. So written, the target is still the right target under any discovery outcome.

**A2 — Provenance-marked grounding.**
*Forbidden:* an unmarked grounding claim; and any **sample-inferred** claim setting a target's comparison tolerance or mandating a mechanism.
*Required instead:* every grounding claim is tagged **source-read** or **sample-inferred**. Inferred claims reach the executor as contestable guidance only. Primary evidence found mid-run that contradicts one escalates as a parent design gap — this is the trigger Bound 2's existing escape hatch was missing.

**A3 — Falsifiers are executor guidance, not check hardening.**
*Forbidden:* converting a known vacuity trap into an assertion or check command the run is graded on. Both trial runs shipped tests that could not fail; in both, the preventing knowledge existed as check-command hardening, and in both it prevented nothing.
*Required instead:* the plan stress-test keeps its strongest catch — targets satisfiable without the fix — but delivers each finding as **prose the brief's fourth section carries to the executor** (`## 4. Falsifiers`), alongside a standing obligation to show that completion evidence could have come out negative.

**A4 — Behavior pins, and a floor check before launch.**
*Forbidden:* a target naming a file path, a test name, or a per-file count ("file X exists"; "the slug module gains at least 6 tests").
*Required instead:* targets pin **observable behavior** — the thing that file or those tests exist to demonstrate. Separately, a target may state an absolute **suite-wide** floor, but only if that floor is verified satisfiable against the launch baseline *before* the run starts; an unchecked floor is forbidden, a checked one is not. Both trial runs were halt-bound from the moment they began because nobody did that arithmetic.

**A5 — Evidence obligations: properties fixed, format free.**
*Forbidden:* an acceptance criterion that names no proof standard, and evidence offered with no properties at all. Free-format-with-no-properties is not a hypothetical failure: both trial runs produced evidence, self-reported green, and carried roughly a dozen defects each.
*Required instead:* every acceptance criterion states what would count as proof, and the proof offered is **discriminating** (it could have come out negative — which for a criterion claiming something new is true means a result that differs from the pre-change tree, and for one claiming something is *preserved* means a check that would have caught the breakage), **attributed** (it says where the number came from: "3,792 randomized paths against a reference implementation", not "the tests pass"), and **per-criterion** (it maps to one criterion, not to one aggregate green). The *format* stays the executor's choice; review is the check on whether the chosen format actually proved the criterion. These three properties' standing home is [skills/verification-before-completion/SKILL.md](../../skills/verification-before-completion/SKILL.md).

A1–A4 govern what the plan **says**. A5 straddles: its first half is also a rule about what the plan says — every criterion must name its proof standard — and its second half is the only rule here about what the **run must show**. All five are rules about how a brief specifies, whichever authority source it carries, so each is true from the moment it lands. (The artifact-independence clause that used to end this sentence is dropped by the scope restatement recorded at the top of this section.)

**What enforcement exists, and what it does not reach.** A1–A5 are applied by the planner and by review. Two things now back that up for a brief: `Invoke-FVPlanValidate` rejects a document declaring itself a brief while carrying none of the sections that shape requires, and the **brief conformance check** (`skills/plan-authoring/SKILL.md` § Brief plan variant) is run by the author before dispatch and by the reviewer as the first act of review — it catches an artifact-pinned criterion (A4), an untagged or over-reaching grounding claim (A2), a criterion naming no proof standard (A5), an unchecked suite floor (A4), and — since #957 chunk 2 added the reading-property — a criteria set carrying a reading under which every criterion passes and no work happens, which is the catch A3 names as the stress-test's strongest. **What none of it reaches: A1, and the rule A3 itself states.** The reach-claim moved here rather than standing unexamined: property 5 performs A3's *catch*, but A3's *prohibition* is about delivery form — a found vacuity trap must reach the executor as prose in the brief's fourth section rather than be hardened into an assertion the run is graded on — and nothing in either mechanism enforces that. It stays a discipline. Neither mechanism is automatic either: structural validation sits in no continuous-integration path, and the conformance check is a reading, not a script — properties needing a judgment about what a criterion *means*, about what the criteria mean *together*, or about a number the text does not contain, cannot be decided mechanically. #924 owns wiring the chunk fast path and provenance marking into `plan-authoring` and `upstream-onboarding`, which is what would make the check fire without someone choosing to run it.
